Diploma, Transcript & Certificate Guide

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything people ask about replacement diplomas, transcripts, GED certificates, and diplomas, answered honestly.

How soon can I receive my diploma?

Digital orders received before 12:00 PM Eastern Time on business days are eligible for same-day processing. Orders received after the cutoff are generally processed the following business day. Printed copies are normally prepared within 1–2 business days before shipping.

How does ordering a custom diploma work?

Choose a diploma and delivery format, enter the college or university information, and place your order. Our production team prepares the layout and, when applicable, a proof for review before printing. Review every single detail before approval.

Can you match my school's diploma design?

We can use an available sample or customer-provided reference to recreate the general layout, parchment appearance, seal placement, and other authentic details of the visual style. We do not promise absolute accuracy or reproduce protected security features for official verification.

Can I use this diploma for official purposes?

No. The diploma is a replica for personal display or commemorative use. It is not an official college degree, certificate, or credential and must not be presented to an employer, school, licensing body, or verification service as genuine.

Can I order diplomas and transcripts together?

Depending on the product, you may request a diploma, academic transcripts, or a complete package. These custom documents are produced from customer-supplied information and are not official academic records.
